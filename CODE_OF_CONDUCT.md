# Code of Conduct

Data Fabric welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers participate.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. They will explain decisions when practical.

To report a concern, use GitHub's report-content feature. To request private follow-up from a maintainer, open an issue titled "Private maintainer contact request" without including sensitive details. Retaliation against good-faith reports is not acceptable.
